Thinking Machines Atlas

123 arguments for and against machine minds. The overview follows the questions the debate asks, in order. Each cluster shows its exchanges: what is contested, the moves on each side, and where the argument goes next.

A companion to the workbook Arguments for and against thinking machines; row IDs match.

Stance is relative to each cluster’s question (in cluster 11, to the claim that AI poses serious risk). The overview is selective: some arguments appear under several questions, and a few appear only in their cluster. The Guide explains every mark and has a glossary.

Created and edited by Daniel Estrada (NJIT). Drafted and built with Claude (Anthropic); reviewed by AI models (see AI on AI). The questions, the placement of arguments under them, and the exchanges are an interpretation, drafted by Claude and edited by Daniel Estrada.
